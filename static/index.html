💡 Tips for Perfect Voice Cloning

  • Duration: 10 to 20 seconds of continuous speech is ideal.
  • Environment: Record in a quiet room with minimal echo and no background music or television.
  • Microphone: Speak clearly at a consistent distance (6–8 inches / 15–20 cm) from the mic.
  • Pacing: Read a natural sentence with normal conversational tone and punctuation pauses.
